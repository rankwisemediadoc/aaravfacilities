Services

Which services do you provide?

Three divisions: manpower and workforce (housekeeping, office and hospitality support, security, logistics staff), facility and workplace support (glazing, hard floors, HVAC and plant), and automotive care (washing, detailing, dealership prep and PPF). You can take one or combine them.

Can I combine services in one agreement?

Yes. Agreements are modular, so you can combine manpower, facility and automotive services across one or more sites under a single agreement and point of contact.

Do you provide security staff?

Yes. Our manpower division supplies security guards and access marshalling as part of a wider staffing arrangement or on its own.

Do you support car dealerships?

Yes. Our automotive division provides car washing, detailing, showroom pre-delivery prep, inventory prep teams, paint protection film and outsourced detailing bay management.

Which industries do you work with?

Manufacturing and industrial sites, corporate offices, automobile dealerships, warehouses and logistics hubs, and commercial properties.

Getting started

How quickly will you respond to an enquiry?

An account director responds within 4 business hours. Our business line is open Monday to Friday, 08:00–19:00 GMT.

Do you visit the site before preparing a proposal?

Yes. We offer a complimentary on-site feasibility audit, including staffing density modelling, so the proposal matches how your site actually runs.

What should I include in my enquiry?

The service you need, the site location, an estimated number of personnel if you know it, and a short description of your requirements. The enquiry form covers all of these.

Do you respond to tenders and pre-qualification requests?

Yes. Contact our enterprise desk about corporate partnerships, tender pre-qualifications, workforce surges and facility SLA enquiries.

Working with us

Can you add staff for seasonal or peak periods?

Yes. Staffing tiers are scaled to shift demand and seasonal spikes, and agreements are modular, so you are not locked into a fixed package.

Are your personnel vetted?

Every team member is vetted before deployment. Our governance is ISO-45001 certified and we maintain full statutory compliance on site.

Who do I contact once the service is running?

You have one point of contact for every site, backed by supervisory audits and unified reporting. Active enterprise contracts also have a 24/7 operations desk.

Where do you operate?

We run multi-site operations from offices in London, Zurich and Singapore. Active enterprise contracts have access to a 24/7 operations desk.
