Four steps from enquiry to a team on site

Every engagement follows the same sequence, whether it covers one site or many.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Tell us what you need

    Send a short brief through the enquiry form or call our business line. An account director replies within 4 business hours.

    Useful to include

    • The service or services you need
    • Site location or city
    • Estimated number of personnel, if known
    • Timelines and any current arrangements
  2. 2

    Step 2: Understand

    We learn your service requirements, operating environment and expectations before deploying any personnel or equipment.

    What happens

    • Complimentary on-site feasibility audit
    • Staffing density modelling for your site
    • Review of compliance and safety requirements
  3. 3

    Step 3: Plan

    We define a practical service scope and line up the people, shift schedules and safety protocols needed to deliver it.

    What you receive

    • A tailored, SLA-backed proposal
    • Modular scope with outcome-based deliverables
    • Shift and staffing plan matched to demand
  4. 4

    Step 4: Support

    Consistent coordination and ongoing communication keep the service running smoothly, responsively and accountably.

    What continues

    • One point of contact for every site
    • Supervisory audits and unified reporting
    • Rapid incident response
    • 24/7 operations desk for active contracts
