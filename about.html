We keep business moving

Every successful business depends on people and operations working together. Aarav Facilities was founded on disciplined execution and operational excellence, and we provide the manpower and support services that help organisations manage day-to-day requirements with more confidence and consistency.

From workplace support and housekeeping to security staffing and automobile care, we build each service around the real needs of the client. We take friction out of daily operations so leadership can put its energy into growth.

Our governance is ISO-45001 certified, our people are vetted before deployment, and we work across manufacturing, corporate, dealership, logistics and commercial sites.

How we approach our work

Four principles every team member works to, on every site.

Reliability

We take service commitments seriously. Our teams show up prepared, on schedule, and work to the agreed operating standards.

Professionalism

A disciplined, respectful approach. Our people reflect your organisation's values in their conduct and presentation.

Flexibility

We adapt the service scope to your requirements and scale teams quickly when demand peaks or changes.

Partnership

We build relationships through communication and accountability, and look after your facilities and assets as if they were our own.
